# Book resource icons

These locally hosted favicons identify the destinations of book information links. They are not generated approximations of the brands and do not imply endorsement.

- `wikipedia.ico`: https://en.wikipedia.org/static/favicon/wikipedia.ico
- `goodreads.ico`: https://www.goodreads.com/favicon.ico

Retrieved 2026-10-02. The marks remain the property of their respective rights holders. Icons are decorative beside visible destination labels; accessible link names also identify the book and new-tab behavior. A small light backplate keeps both originals legible against the dark card background.
